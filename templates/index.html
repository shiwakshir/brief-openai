02 / What AI Assumes

What AI assumes about this topic

03 / What AI Misses

What AI gets wrong about your audience

What AI leans on too heavily, what it skips, and the questions neither the client nor AI is asking.

04 / Client Hypotheses

Are the client's hypotheses actually theirs?

How much of what your client "knows" came from AI rather than the market. A high score means test the assumption before you build research around it.

05 / Temporal Drift

How stale is AI's picture of this market?

AI training data stops at a point in time. Markets keep moving. These are the assumptions most likely to be out of date.

06 / Brand Priming

Who AI puts in the room before you do

Brands that show up in AI answers without being asked for. Your respondents have probably run into them too.

07 / Where It Comes From

08 / How to Research It

Method recommendations based on the specific gaps above.

09 / What AI Actually Said

The raw AI answers behind every score

Six consumer-style questions and four persona variants, asked to each probe model, exactly as answered. Every score in this report is computed from these.

10 / Paste-ready Outputs
